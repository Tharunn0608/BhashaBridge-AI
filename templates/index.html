How it works

  1. 1

    Speak

    Choose a language and tap the mic. Your browser records a short clip.

  2. 2

    Transcribe

    The clip goes to our server, then to Gnani Prisma which returns the text.

  3. 3

    Reason

    A reasoning model, or the student FAQ, writes a helpful answer.

  4. 4

    Listen

    Gnani Timbre turns the answer into natural speech you can replay.

Features

Multilingual voice

English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil and Telugu, all supported by both Prisma and Timbre.

Context-aware chat

Follow-up questions work. History is saved locally and can be cleared any time.

Student assistance

Searchable FAQ on courses, exams, campus life and study help. Demo data you can edit.

Type or speak

Prefer typing? Every feature works with text too.

Replay and copy

Replay any answer in audio or copy the text with one tap.

Private by design

No keys in the browser. Voice clips are processed in memory and never saved.
