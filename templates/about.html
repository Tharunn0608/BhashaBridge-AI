About

Voice AI that speaks your language

BhashaBridge AI is a multilingual voice assistant for Indian students and anyone who prefers to communicate in their regional language.

Project objective

Many students are most comfortable in their mother tongue, yet most digital help is English-first. BhashaBridge AI lets them speak a question, get a clear written answer, and hear it back, lowering the barrier to information.

It was built for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by Gnani AI as a practical demonstration of multilingual voice processing.

Gnani AI integration

  • Prisma (speech-to-text): POST /stt/v3 on the Vachana API, with the language you choose. Supports English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil and Telugu (among others).
  • Timbre (text-to-speech): POST /api/v1/tts/inference with a language-matched voice from the official voice catalog.
  • Reasoning: any OpenAI-compatible model you configure (for example a self-hosted Gnani Evon), with a demonstration FAQ fallback. Every reply is labelled with its source.

Only languages documented as supported by both Prisma and Timbre are enabled. This project is independent and is not endorsed by Gnani.ai.

Technology stack

The browser records audio, converts it to 16 kHz mono WAV in memory, and sends it to the Flask backend. The backend is the only place the API key exists.

Privacy and limits

  • Voice clips are held in memory only for the request and are never written to disk by this app.
  • Conversation text is stored locally in SQLite and can be cleared with the Clear button.
  • FAQ content is demonstration data, not real college policy, dates or fees.
  • AI answers can be wrong. Verify important information with your college or official sources.
