Most students think in their mother tongue. Most digital help doesn't speak it. 🎙️

For the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by Gnani AI, I built **BhashaBridge AI**, a multilingual voice assistant for Indian students.

You speak in English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil or Telugu →
🗣️ **Gnani Prisma** turns your speech into text
🧠 A reasoning layer writes a helpful answer in your language
🔊 **Gnani Timbre** speaks the answer back in a natural voice

What I'm proud of:
• A student help section with a searchable FAQ (clearly labelled demo data)
• Context-aware follow-up questions and conversation history
• Privacy by design: API keys stay on the server, voice clips are never stored
• Honest by default: every answer shows whether it came from an AI model or the FAQ fallback

Built with Python, Flask, SQLite and plain JavaScript. Biggest lesson: browsers record audio in formats speech APIs don't always accept, so I convert recordings to 16 kHz WAV in the browser before upload.
